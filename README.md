<h1>Design Cart Spin To Win – 3D Prize Wheel for WooCommerce 🎡</h1>
<p><strong>Turn ordinary discount campaigns into an interactive shopping experience.</strong></p>
<p>Design Cart Spin To Win is a free WordPress plugin for WooCommerce that lets customers spin a three-dimensional prize wheel, win a promotional coupon, and receive their discount code by email.</p>
<p>Instead of showing another static discount banner, let customers take part in your campaign. The wheel is powered by <strong>Three.js and WebGL</strong>, featuring realistic 3D elements, lighting effects, animated spinning, and mouse/touch interaction.</p>
<p>The plugin integrates directly with native WooCommerce coupons, allowing store owners to manage promotional rewards, configure popup behavior, customize typography, collect email submissions, and export campaign data.</p>

<h2>The Story Behind the Plugin</h2>
<p>Spin To Win was originally developed by Paweł Nosko at Design Cart for a client's Black Friday campaign on PrestaShop.</p>
<p>The idea was simple: make discount campaigns more engaging by replacing ordinary promotional popups with an interactive prize wheel.</p>
<p>Following the original PrestaShop implementation, the concept was adapted for WooCommerce, with a dedicated WordPress administration interface, native WooCommerce coupon integration, AJAX-based prize claims, and email delivery.</p>
<p>Both versions were created for Design Cart clients' Black Friday campaigns.</p>
